Languages

  • English native
  • Romanian C1
  • Chinese B2

Over the last four years I’ve been obsessed with learning Chinese — enough to uproot my life and end up with two home bases. I’ve grown a deep appreciation for the culture, and I want to keep showing that there are more ways to live than the one we’re used to back in the West. Lots of Chinese content is in the works.

Adventures and discomfort

In 2026 alone: running my first marathon, my first thru-hike (250 km), and cycling 1,000 km around Taiwan — years of passion for the outdoors and pushing my body to find its limit. As I’m starting to outgrow typical travel checklists and tourist locations, I’m on a mission to see how far I can go and show what we’re capable of.
