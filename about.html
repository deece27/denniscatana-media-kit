About the Creator

Dennis Catana

English-language travel and endurance creator based in China — documenting multi-day bike journeys, high-altitude treks, and everyday cultural discoveries across Asia and beyond.

The Story & Format

Challenge-Driven Storytelling

Whether cycling 1,000km around Taiwan, trekking the Salkantay Pass to Machu Picchu on a DJI Osmo Action 6, or tackling the Hong Kong 4 Trails, every series is built as a day-by-day narrative that hooks cold viewers and converts them into followers.

Based in China, Filming Globally

Living in China provides constant access to under-documented street culture, regional trains, and mountain trails — resulting in organic Reels spikes of 9.3M, 2.9M, and 1.5M views alongside international endurance trips in Peru, Korea, and Taiwan.
